Core Principles

Why Use MediFind?

Built specifically for patient trust, transparency, and medical safety.

Verified Pharmacies Only

Pharmacies undergo manual verification of state drug licenses and pharmacist registration certificates.

Inventory Transparency

Every stock item shows an exact "Last updated: X minutes ago" timestamp to prevent false real-time expectations.

100% Manual Rx Review

No AI diagnosis or automated prescription approvals. All prescriptions are verified manually by licensed pharmacists.

Nearby Pharmacy Map

Discover pharmacies within 5km, 10km, or 25km with accurate Haversine distance calculations.

Pickup & Delivery Options

Choose whether to reserve for quick counter pickup or request home delivery where supported by local pharmacies.

Order Lifecycle Tracking

Follow your order status in real time through confirmed, preparing, ready, and completed stages.

Important Notice: Medicine availability information depends on pharmacy inventory updates and may not represent real-time stock. Always confirm availability with the pharmacy when necessary. MediFind does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or clinical decision-making.
